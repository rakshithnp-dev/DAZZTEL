# HOTELYO - Platform Administrator User Guide

## 1. Property & Inventory Management
- **Properties:** Create and configure hostel properties, tax settings (GSTIN), operating time zone, and cancellation policies.
- **Rooms & Beds:** Configure room layouts (dormitories, private rooms), bed numbers, and segment classifications (`STANDARD` vs `MOTORCYCLE_RESERVED`).
- **Motorcycle Inventory:** Configure motorcycle bed allocation ratio (default: 50%) and auto-release cutoff hours before check-in.
- **Resource Inventory:** Manage bookable bike parking spaces and secure gear lockers.

## 2. Analytics & Reporting (Option B)
- **Occupancy Metrics:** Track occupancy percentage across selectable date ranges, bed utilization rates, and dorm vs private room split.
- **Revenue Analytics:**
  - Gross Booking Value vs. Net Recognized Revenue.
  - Accommodation Revenue vs. Ancillary Revenue (food, laundry, parking, lockers).
  - Total Refunds and Cancellation counts.
- **Loyalty Ledger & FAQ Management:**
  - Create and publish FAQ categories and articles.
  - Audit guest loyalty point ledger entries and process authorized adjustments.
